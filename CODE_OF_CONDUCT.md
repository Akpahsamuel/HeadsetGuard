# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in HeadsetGuard to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or nationality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, and keep it about the code, not the person.
- Help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Publishing others' private information
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report problems by contacting the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Akpahsamuel). Reports will be handled privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
